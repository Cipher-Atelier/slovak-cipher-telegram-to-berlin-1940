# Berlin HCP128: residual evidence audit, 9 October 2026

The completed independent replay of the saved evidence passed. It confirms consistency of the saved computation, not a complete decipherment or the correctness of every source sign.

The support thresholds belong to different saved versions. Support of at least four belongs to the frozen baseline. Support of at least three belongs to the corrected version after the source correction at position 327 from m to n. Both statements are retained with their respective versions; merging them would misstate the evidence. A correction learned after evaluation is not a new blind test.

The fixed-key reading remains partial. Source ambiguities, masks and unused alphabet values remain open, along with the limits arising from prior exposure to the full image. No new letters were established by the residual audit, and no fresh holdout was created.
